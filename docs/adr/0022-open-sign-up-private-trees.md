# 0022 Open Sign-up, Private Trees

Anyone with a Google account can sign up, with no allowlist or approval (LIN-85). An emailed sign-in link is a later upgrade. A new account with no invite gives their name and gets a Person and a renamable Tree named after them, as its Master Admin, and lands on the canvas showing only them. An account that arrives by invite is bound and goes straight to that Tree, with "Start your own Tree" and a Tree switcher in a menu.

No Tree is ever public or searchable, there are no public profiles, and a Tree is reached only by invite. Nobody is told that they or a relative appear on another Tree. Who can invite stays the 1-Degree Network rule, plus Admins.

An account can be Master Admin of one Tree and a Member of any number, and a Tree can hold 5,000 Persons. The Owner can lift either limit.

## Considered Options

- **Approval or an allowlist before sign-up.** Rejected: Osra is a public product.
- **Up to 3 Trees per account.** Rejected: the Owner chose one.

## Consequences

- Two people can record the same relatives on separate Trees and never find out.
